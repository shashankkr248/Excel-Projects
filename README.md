# Excel Projects

A collection of Excel data analytics and business intelligence projects focused on sales analysis, retail performance, customer analytics, product analysis, KPI reporting, trend analysis and business dashboards.

## Projects

### 1. Amazon Sales Analysis

An Amazon sales dashboard analyzing total sales, units sold, city-wise sales, state-wise sales and product-level unit performance.

**Tools:** Excel, PivotTables, PivotCharts, Slicers, Data Visualization

[View Project](https://github.com/shashankkr248/Excel-Projects/tree/main/Amazon%20Sales%20Analysis)
