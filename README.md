# Excel Projects

A collection of Excel data analytics and business intelligence projects covering sales performance, retail, customer analytics, product analysis and annual sales reporting.

## Projects

### 1. Amazon Sales Analysis

Amazon sales analytics dashboard analyzing total sales, units sold, city-wise sales, state-wise sales and product-wise unit performance.

Tools: Excel, PivotTables, PivotCharts, Slicers, Data Visualization

[View Project](<./Amazon Sales Analysis>)

### 2. Deepak Sales Performance Analysis

Sales performance dashboard analyzing total sales, units sold, payment modes, top-selling products, sales trends, seasonal order patterns and top sellers.

Tools: Excel, PivotTables, PivotCharts, Slicers, Trend Analysis

[View Project](<./Deepak Sales Performance Analysis>)

### 3. DMart Sales Analysis

Retail sales dashboard analyzing category-wise sales, sub-category performance, regional sales, state-level performance, customer segments and shipping modes.

Tools: Excel, PivotTables, PivotCharts, Slicers, Timeline

[View Project](<./DMart Sales Analysis>)

### 4. McDonald's Sales Analysis

Sales and customer performance dashboard analyzing sales, profit, customer volume, customer satisfaction, monthly sales trends and country-level performance.

Tools: Excel, PivotTables, PivotCharts, KPI Analysis, Data Visualization

[View Project](<./McDonald's Sales Analysis>)

### 5. Sales Analytics Dashboard

Business sales analytics dashboard analyzing revenue, margins, new and repeat customers, customer sources, sales by city, service types, department margins and top customers.

Tools: Excel, PivotTables, PivotCharts, Slicers, KPI Analysis, Trend Analysis

[View Project](<./Sales Analytics Dashboard>)

### 6. Vrinda Sales Annual Report Analysis

Annual sales analytics dashboard analyzing monthly sales, gender demographics, age groups, top-performing states, sales channels, product categories and order status.

Tools: Excel, PivotTables, PivotCharts, Slicers, Data Visualization

[View Project](<./Vrinda Sales Annual Report Analysis>)
